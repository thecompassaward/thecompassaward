Why we started

Every patient needs a compass. For many, it's their PA.

Between us, we have spent decades caring for patients in hospital medicine, primary care and specialty clinics. Over those years, patients have told us the same thing in many different words: thank you for helping me make sense of it all.

Modern care is rarely one clinician and one plan. A parent coordinates a child's surgeon, physical therapist and school nurse. An older adult leaves the hospital with new medications and three follow-up visits. Someone with a new diagnosis meets a different specialist every few weeks. It is easy to feel lost between appointments.

Again and again, it is a PA who helps them find their way. PAs are trained as medical generalists and work in nearly every specialty, so we often see the whole picture. We explain the plan in plain words, call back with results, catch what fell between visits, and connect each member of the team to the next. Physicians, nurses and many others do this work too. PAs are often the continuity that holds it together.

Patients notice, and they want to say so. For more than 25 years, patients have had a national way to thank the nurses who cared for them. PAs have never had one. So we built the ComPAss Award: a way for patients to recognize the PA who helped them find their way, and for that quiet, essential work to finally be seen.

How it works

Simple for your team. Unforgettable for your PAs.

STEP 1

PA is nominated

Patients, families and colleagues share their stories through your own nomination page, QR posters or paper forms.

STEP 2

Your team chooses

A small committee reads blinded nominations and scores them with our four-point rubric.

STEP 3

Everyone celebrates

A surprise ceremony on the unit. The nomination is read aloud, and the honoree is pinned with their ComPAss pin.

Four points of the compass

What we honor

  • N · NORTH

    Compassion

    The patient or family felt truly seen and cared for.

  • E · EAST

    Clinical excellence

    Sound judgment that ensured excellence of care.

  • S · SOUTH

    Communication

    Clear explanations, real listening, hard decisions made together.

  • W · WEST

    Teamwork

    Supports colleagues through mentorship, teaching, and advocacy.

Questions

Frequently asked questions

Nurses, including NPs and other advanced practice nurses, already have awards of their own. Your PAs deserve one too.

What is the ComPAss Award?

A recognition program for PAs: physician assistants and physician associates. Patients, families and colleagues nominate PAs who gave extraordinary, compassionate care. Each partner hospital or practice chooses its own honorees and celebrates them on the unit.

Who is eligible?

Licensed, practicing PAs at a partner organization, in any specialty or setting, full or part time. PAs in leadership roles who still see patients are eligible too.

Why just PAs, and not all APPs?

Nurses, including nurse practitioners and other advanced practice nurses, already have established recognition awards. PAs don't. The ComPAss Award gives PAs equivalent recognition so no one on your APP team is left out.

What does it cost?

Pricing is one yearly package based on how many PAs you have, with honoree kits included: $900 for 1 to 9 PAs, $2,500 for 10 to 99, and $4,500 for 100 or more. Founding partners save a third or more ($500, $1,500 or $3,000) and keep that rate for 3 years as long as they renew each year. See For hospitals.

What's in an honoree kit?

A ComPAss lapel pin, a framed certificate and a printed copy of the nomination.

How much work is it for our team?

About 2 to 4 hours a month for a coordinator, plus an hour per selection round for a small committee. We provide the nomination page, materials and templates.

How are honorees chosen?

Your committee scores blinded nominations using our rubric, built on the four points of the compass: compassion, clinical excellence, communication and teamwork. Compassion counts double.

How do you protect patient privacy?

Nominations stay with your team. The form asks nominators whether their story may be read aloud or shared, and no story is shared publicly without permission and without patient details removed. We recommend your privacy office review the form during launch.

Can practices and urgent care centers join?

Yes. Pricing is based on how many PAs you have, not the type of organization, so a 4-PA urgent care and a 120-PA specialty group both fit.

How can we pay for it?

Most partners use an APP department budget or a hospital foundation. A local business or civic group can sponsor an award. We don't accept sponsorship from drug or device companies, because PAs are covered recipients under federal Open Payments rules.

Who runs the ComPAss Award?

The ComPAss Award was created by practicing PAs and is run by a small independent company. Partner organizations pay a yearly fee to run the program, much as hospitals do for other recognition programs. We never sell awards to individuals, and only your team chooses honorees.
